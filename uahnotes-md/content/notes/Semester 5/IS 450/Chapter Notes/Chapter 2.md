## Planning

- The following documents:
  - Mission Statement
    - What the organization does and for whom
  - Vision Statement
    - What the organization wants to become
      - Where the organization wants to go, while the mission statement describes how it wants to get there
      - Should be ambitious
  - Values statement
    - Formal set of organization principles and qualities
    - Benchmarks for measuring behavior against these published values
    - Conduct and performance standards are clear to employees and public

### Strategic Planning

- **Strategic Planning:** The process of defining and specifying the long-term direction an organization will take, as well as the acquisition and location of resources needed to pursue that effort
  - Identify areas of improvement
  - Document current position relative to goal
  - Make a plan for how to achieve the goal
    ![](../assets/2225b00b551a1704b75ec1f5ea914ac9.png)

#### Creating a strategic plan

- Extends general strategy into specific plans for each division
- Each division translates objectives into more detailed goals for the level below
- Executives must align goals across levels, understand unit capabilities and collaborate with peers, superiors and subordinates

#### Planning Levels

- SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant and Time-bound)
- Strategic plans are used to create tactical plans, used to develop operational plans
- Tactical planning has more short-term and strategic planning
  - Breaks applicable strategic goals into series of incremental objectives
- Operational planning is used by managers and employees to organize ongoing, day-to-day performance of tasks
  - Includes identified coordination activities across department boundaries, communications requirements, weekly meetings, progress reports,etc
    ![](../assets/d576022bc917c9d12158386b898e3551.png)

#### Planning and CSO

- Basic components or a strategic plan should include following
  - Executive summary
  - Mission, vision, values statement
  - Organizational profile and history
  - Strategic issues and challenges
  - Organizational goals and objectives 
  - Major business unit goals and objectives
  - Appendices and research and development protectives

## Cybersecurity Governance

- Strategic planning and corporate responsibility are best accomplished using approach industry refers to Governance, Risk Management, Compliance (GRC)
- Governance is the set of responsibilities and practices exercised by board and executive management with the following goals
  - Provide strategic direction
  - Ensure that objectives are achieved
  - Ascertain that risks are managed appropriately 
- Must be objectives must be addressed at the highest levels of an organization's management team in order to be effective and offer a sustainable approach
- When security programs are designed and managed as a technical specialty within IT, less likely to be effective
  - Broader view of cybersecurity encompasses all of an organization's information assets, including IT assets

### Industry Approaches to Cybersecurity Governance

- ISACA says cybersecurity governance includes accountabilities and methods undertaken by the board of directors and executive management to provide the following
  - Strategic direction
  - Establishment of objectives
  - Measurement of progress towards those objectives
  - Risk management practices are appropriate
  - Validation that organization assets are used properly

### Desired outcomes

- Following are desired outcomes of cybersecurity governance
  - Strategic alignment
  - Risk management
  - Resource management
  - Performance measurement
  - Value delivery

### Benefits of Governance

- Increase of share value
- Increased predictability and reduced uncertainty
- Protection from increasing potential for civil and legal liability as a result of information inaccuracy or absence of due care
- Assurance of cyber policy compliance
- When developing a cyber program, designers should ensure the following:
  - Cybersecurity risk management methodology
  - Comprehensive security strategy linked business and IT objectives
  - Effective security organizational structure
  - Security strategy that talks about the value of information being protected and delivered
  - Security policies that address each aspect of strategy, control and regulation
  - Complete set of security standards for each policy to ensure that procedures and guidelines comply with policies
  - Institutionalized monitoring processes to ensure compliance and provide feedback on effectiveness and mitigation of risk
  - Process to ensure continued evaluation and updating of security policies, standards, procedures and risks

## Governance of Information Security

ISO 27014:2020 standard specifies the following high-level action-oriented cybersecurity governance principles:

1. Establish integrated entity-wide information security
2. Make decisions using a risk-based approach
3. Set direction of acquisitions
4. Ensure conformance with internal and external requirements
5. Foster a security-positive culture
6. Ensure security performance meets current and future requirements
   ![](../assets/813ed2535dd52649bbcf6aeb4004899e.png)

## GRC Squared

- Two acronyms:
  - Goals, Requirements, and Constraints
  - Governance, Risk Management, Compliance
- Governance ensures an organization is directed and controlled toward achieving its goals
- Risk management involves anticipating potential risks
- Well-defined process and consistent accounting of an organizations practices to ensure adherence to policies, standards and guidelines

## Security Convergence

- Deloitte report on convergence commissioned by the Alliance for Enterprise Security Risk Management identified the key approaches organizations use achieve unified enterprise risk management (ERM)
  - Combining physical security and cybersecurity
  - Using separate business functions
  - Using a risk council approach to provide collaborative method for risk management

## Implementing the Security Program Using SDLC approach

- The **Systems development life cycle**
  - Methodology for design and implementation
    - Phases: investigation, analysis, design, implementation, and maintenance of the system
- Once implemented, should be supported via continuous improvement (CIP) 
- Several SDLC methodologies can guide cybersecurity program development and implementation
  ![](../assets/178e8f974e98fa9ee823eaddebfa0b60.png)

### Investigation in SDLC

- Investigation phase
  - Directive from management specifying process, outcomes, and goals of project
  - Budget
  - Frequently begins with affirmation 
- Teams are assembled to investigate problems, scope, goals, constraints
- Feasibility analysis determines whether the organization has resources the resources and commitment a successful analysis and design

### Analysis in the SDLC

- Preliminary analysis of existing policies or programs
  - Analysis of relevant issues 
- Risk management begins here
- Teams assess relative risk for each information asset via risk assessment process 

### Design in the SDLC

- Two distinct places
  - Logical design phase - create blueprint for security
  - Physical design phase - evaluate the technology needed to support the security blueprint
- **Cybersecurity policy:** provides guidance and requirements for protecting information assets of an organization
- **SETA program:** control measure designed to reduce accidental security breaches by employees
- Formulation of controls and safeguards - security mechanisms, policies, or procedures to counter attacks, reduce attacks, reduce risk, resolve vulnerabilities
- **Controls implemented:** managerial, operational and technical
- **Prepared document:**
  - Business continuity plans (BC plans)
  - Disaster Recovery Plans (DR plans)
  - Incident Response Plans (IR plans)
  - The make up Contingency Plans

### Implementation in the SDLC

- Security solutions are acquired, tested, implemented, and retested 
  - Training and education programs are conducted
- Perhaps the most important element of the implementation phase is **execution of the project plan**
  1. Planning the project
  2. Supervising the tasks and action steps within the project
  3. Wrapping up the project

### Maintenance in the SDLC

- Constant monitoring, testing, modifying, updating and repairing
- Constant adaption to prevent threats from successfully penetrating sensitive data
- Intended to complement a systems management model and focus ongoing maintenance efforts needed to keep systems useable and secure
