- Focus of cybersecurity is protecting **information assets** - resources that have value to the organization
- Cybersecurity must involve three distinct groups of managers:
	- Those in the field of cyber
	- Field of IT
	- Rest of org
- CIA Triad
	- Confidentiality, integrity, availability
- CIA triad has expanded to include privacy, authenticity, possession and utility
- Systems use access control processes like identification, authentication, authorization and accountability (IAAA)
- ![](../assets/07a09e6e741e5e86f2d5ec9a45763955.png)
- **Confidentiality** means limiting access to information only to those who need it and preventing access by those who do not
- **Integrity** describes how data is whole, complete, and uncorrupted
	- Integrity of information is threatened when it is exposed to corruption, damage, destruction or other disruption of its authentic state
- **Availability** is how data is accessible and correctly formatted for use without interference or obstruction
	- Information means that users, people or other systems have access to it in a usable format
	- Does not imply that accessible to any user - can be authorized only
- **Privacy** is the right of individuals or groups to protect themselves and their information from unauthorized access, providing confidentiality
## IAAA Framework

### Identification
- Unverified entities who seek access to a resource provide a label by which the system knows who they are
### Authentication
- Access control mechanism that requires validation and verification of an unauthenticated entities purported identity
- Process by which a control establishes whether a user has the identity it claims to have
### Accountability
- Access control mechanism that ensures all actions on a system can be attributed to an authenticated entity
### Authorization
- Access control mechanism that represents the matching of authenticated entity to a list of info assets and access levels
- After auth completed, checking that the user can access the requested resources

## General Cyber concepts
- **Threat:** a risk to an info asset
- **Attack (threat event):** represents an ongoing act against the asset that could result in a loss
- **Threat agent:** attempts to damage or seal an organizations information or physical assets by using an exploit
- **Exploit:** a technique used to compromise a system to take advantage of a vulnerability
- **Vulnerability:** potential weakness in an asset or its defensive control system

### Data and Asset Compromise
- Intellectual property (IP) can be trade secrets, copyrights, trademarks, and patents 
	- Software piracy
	- User registration
- Physical theft
- Electronic theft
- Information extortion (ransomware,etc)

### Human and Operational Threats
- Human error or failure
	- Can be prevented with training
- Sabotage or Vandalism
	- Deliberate sabotage of computer system or business
	- Can include
		- Online activism
		- Cyberterrorism
		- Positive activism
- Espionage or Trespass
	- Unauthorized access to confidential date or physical premises
		- Password cracking 
		- Jailbreaking or rooting
## System and Operational vulnerabilities
- Technical hardware failures or errors 
- Failure measurement: 
	- **MTBF** - mean time between failure
		- Assumes that can be repaired/returned to service
	- **MTTF** - mean time to failure
		- Assumes that item must be replaced
- Technological obsolescence
	- Antiquated/outdated infrastructure
- Deviations in services
	- Irregularities in internet service, communication, psu
	- **Service level agreement**
- Technical software failures or errors
	- Broken access controls,etc
- Forces of Nature
- Malicious software attacks
	- Modern threat vectors leveraging AI

## Management Characteristics
- **Management** is the process of achieving objectives using a given set of resources 
- **Leadership** is the ability to influence others to gain their willing and achieve an objective by providing purpose, direction, and motivation
	- Autocratic - decision making is theirs
	- Democratic - seek input from others
	- Laissez-faire - sit back
- ![](../assets/3ef990a3d9779869f25dd74cb4102020.png)
- **Planning** is the process of creating designs or schemes for future efforts or performance
- **Organizing** is the management function dedicated to the structuring of resources to support the accomplishment of objectives 
- **Leading** encourages the implementation of the planning and organizing functions, and includes supervising employee behavior, performance, attendance, and attitude
- **Controlling** ensures the validity of the organization’s plan
- **Governance:** set of responsibilities and practices exercised by the board and executive management with the goal of providing strategic direction, ensuring objectives are achieved, etc.
